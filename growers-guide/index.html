01 / OVERVIEW

Build it in veg.

The canopy you flip is the canopy you’re going to finish with. Once plants move into flower, you’re mostly working with the structure built during veg.

That’s why this program starts early. Apply Step 1 — Catalyst three to five days after transplant, once the plants are moving again. Follow with Step 2 — Amplify five to seven days later as new nodes and branches develop.

In fast rooms, both applications are finished before a day 10–14 flip. If you veg longer, continue alternating Step 1 — Catalyst and Step 2 — Amplify every five to seven days until you’re ready to flip.

03 / THE STEPS

What each step does.

Prime application

Step 1 — Catalyst

Prime timing: 3–5 days after transplant.

Give the plants a few days to settle after transplant. By day three to five, rooted cuttings should be moving water on their own and putting transplant stress behind them.

That is the window for Step 1 — Catalyst. The aim is simple: get the batch moving evenly. Small differences early in veg tend to become bigger differences later.

Day 3–5 is the best window, not a hard cutoff. If you miss it, apply Step 1 — Catalyst when you can and keep the usual five-to-seven-day interval before Step 2 — Amplify.

Prime application

Step 2 — Amplify

Prime timing: 5–7 days after Step 1 — Catalyst.

Apply Step 2 — Amplify once the plants are clearly growing again and adding nodes. This is when that early growth starts turning into the branches and tops the plants will carry into flower.

Step 1 — Catalyst gets the batch moving. Step 2 — Amplify follows as the canopy starts to build.

04 / APPLICATION

Light coverage. No mixing.

Shake, spray and keep the rest of the room on its normal program.

Less is more

Shake well. Apply a light, even spray until the foliage is lightly damp—not to runoff. Full top-and-bottom coverage is not required. Do not dilute.

Time it to the environment

Outdoors, spray in the early morning or late afternoon. Indoors, spray at lights-off.

Apply alone

Do not combine either product with other products in the same sprayer. No waiting period is required before or after separately applied foliar sprays, including pesticides.

Treat by batch

In perpetual rooms, count from each batch's own transplant date. There is no need to change your base nutrients, EC, irrigation strategy, dryback targets or defoliation schedule.

Size the order

Run one representative batch, note how many plants a bottle covers and use that number for future orders. Later applications may use more product because the plants are larger.

Store correctly

Store cool and out of direct light. Keep bottles upright and tightly closed. Lock the sprayer trigger when it is not in use.

Why the cadence matters

Ready-to-use bottles keep the application consistent between operators and batches. There are no scales, dilution ratios or mixing calls. Whoever has the sprayer can run the same application the same way.

05 / MOTHER PLANTS

For mother plants.

Mother plants can stay on the same alternating schedule. After taking cuttings, give the plant Step 1 — Catalyst while it recovers, then follow with Step 2 — Amplify as the new shoots come in.

  1. Apply Step 1 — Catalyst within one to two days after taking cuttings.
  2. Apply Step 2 — Amplify five to seven days later during regrowth.
  3. Keep alternating the two products every five to seven days.

On a 10–14-day cutting cycle, that works out to about one application per week.

06 / FIELD NOTES

When the schedule changes.

I missed the Step 1 — Catalyst window. It is day 8.

Apply Step 1 — Catalyst when you can, then apply Step 2 — Amplify five to seven days later. Day three to five is the best window, but day eight is not too late.

I sprayed on day one, before the plants were moving water on their own.

Stay on schedule. Apply Step 2 — Amplify five to seven days later, once the plants are moving and growing again.

My veg is only ten days. Does the program fit?

Yes. Apply Step 1 — Catalyst on day three and Step 2 — Amplify five days later. Both applications are finished by day eight.

My veg is six weeks.

Apply Step 1 — Catalyst, followed by Step 2 — Amplify five to seven days later. Continue alternating the two products every five to seven days until the plants are ready to flip.

Can I apply it again later in veg?

Yes. Keep alternating Step 1 — Catalyst and Step 2 — Amplify every five to seven days until the plants are ready to flip.

I top, train or run sea of green.

Keep doing what works in your room. These products do not replace topping, training, spacing or trellising.

Half my batch was transplanted Monday and half Thursday.

Treat them as two batches and count from each transplant date. Do not combine the spray days just to make the calendar easier.

Can I combine it with another product in the same sprayer?

No. Spray each product on its own. There is no required waiting period before or after other foliar sprays, including pesticides.

07 / BOUNDARIES

What it is not for.

Cuttings under dome

Wait until they are out of the dome and moving water on their own.

Flowering plants

This program is for veg, not flower.

Rescue treatment

It will not correct problems with the environment, irrigation, pests or nutrition.

For use on legally cultivated crops in accordance with all applicable state and local regulations. Not a pesticide, fungicide or plant growth regulator. Keep out of reach of children.
